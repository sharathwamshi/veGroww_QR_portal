{# ======================= Privacy & control ======================= #}
For you

Your work stays yours

  • Private by default. Only you can see, edit or delete what you create — other users can't, even with a link to your editor.
  • Public only where it should be. The page a QR opens (your vCard, invite or verification page) is public, because that's what it's for.
  • Always know your allowance. Every tool shows how many you've used and what's left.
  • Self-hosted. Photos, logos and generated files are stored on the organisation's own server.
For administrators

Full control over access

  • Approve who joins — or open sign-ups to everyone, or close them.
  • Switch tools on or off for each person.
  • Set limits per tool and overall, counted in total or per month.
  • Defaults for new users, so every new account starts with the right setup.
{# ======================= FAQ ======================= #}
Questions

Good to know

Can other people see the QR codes I create?

No. Your lists, editors and downloads are visible only to you. The only public part is the page a QR code opens — for example your vCard or invitation — so the people who scan it can see it.

What happens when I reach my limit?

The portal tells you clearly and stops new items in that tool. Depending on how your account is set up, deleting something frees a slot, or your allowance resets at the start of the next month. Your administrator can raise it.

If I edit a vCard or invitation, do I need to reprint the QR?

No. The QR points at your page's link, which never changes, so edits appear on the same code straight away.

Do UPI codes work with every payment app?

Yes. They use the standard UPI format, so GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and bank apps can read them directly.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Ask your administrator — they can set a new one for you from the Users screen. You can then change it yourself from My account.
